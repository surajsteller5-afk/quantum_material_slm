# Host Dependence of Exchange Coupling

## Key Insight
Exchange coupling distance depends on defect wavefunction size.

- Phosphorus donors in silicon: large Bohr radius → J = 300 MHz at close separation, 12 MHz at 15-22 nm.
- NV centers in diamond: small Bohr radius → exchange only at sub-nm.

## Consequence for Direction B
- Diamond/SiC/hBN hybrid pairs require sub-nm placement (30-50× beyond current capability).
- Silicon donor qubits can achieve exchange at 10-20 nm, within current fabrication.

## Revised Direction B Search
Look for bright room-temperature optical antennas in silicon that can be paired with phosphorus donor spins.

## Next Step
Search for silicon single-photon emitters (G centers, T centers, rare-earth ions) with high Debye-Waller factor and dipole moment.
