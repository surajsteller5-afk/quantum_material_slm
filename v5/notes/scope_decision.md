# Scope Decision: Single-Defect SOC-Free to Exchange-Coupled Hybrid

## Original Aim
Find a single defect with strong spin-photon coupling at room temperature, ideally without relying on spin-orbit coupling.

## Finding
Single-defect SOC-free electric-dipole coupling faces a DW-dipole trade-off. No candidate passed quantitative screening.

## Decision
Pivot to Direction B: exchange-coupled hybrid system.

## New Architecture
- Spin-bearing site: weak SOC, long T2 at 300 K.
- Optical antenna site: high DW, strong dipole (SiV⁻-class).
- Coupling between them: exchange or dipole-dipole, independent of SOC.

## Consequence
This is a different claim: room-temperature spin-photon coupling via a two-site hybrid, not a single SOC-free defect.
